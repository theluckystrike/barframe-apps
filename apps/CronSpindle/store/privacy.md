CronSpindle Privacy Policy

CronSpindle processes job names, expressions, time zones, assumed durations and resource labels entirely on your Mac. Saved plans remain in the application sandbox. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, telemetry, cloud services or automatic network requests. We collect no data.

Sample fixtures are separate from the live plan. Delete local plan in Settings removes the saved plan. Copy explanation writes the displayed analysis to the system clipboard only when you request it. Other apps may access clipboard contents under system rules.

Support and Privacy Policy links open your browser at https://barframe.com/apps/cronspindle/ . Normal browser network requests are outside the app. Launch at login is optional and initially off. No file, calendar, automation or Accessibility access is requested.
