# ShelfDock Privacy Policy

Michael Lip provides ShelfDock. Shelf data is saved locally in the app sandbox container: file names, security-scoped bookmarks, groups, dates, complete text snippets, and dropped image data. File entries reference originals rather than copying them. The app reads user-selected files for requested previews, copying, dragging, or sharing. It does not scan your disk, require an account, collect analytics, or perform background uploads.

Clipboard text is read only when you choose a clipboard action. Sample mode uses bundled neutral example files and a separate temporary store. Live shelf data stays separate; leaving Sample restores it. Any additions or removals made in Sample are discarded when you leave. No login preference is enabled by default; system login-item status controls that setting.

Remove an item or clear the shelf to delete its saved payload or reference, without deleting the original file. Accepted drags can retain recoverable items for Undo when automatic clearing is enabled; Clear Shelf deletes this Undo history too. Preview exports are temporary files inside the container cache and are removed when their preview window closes. Container preferences and cached data may survive deleting the app itself. Removing the app container removes its local data.

Sharing is always user initiated through system services. Depending on the service you select, it can transfer your data to another device or service. Policy and support buttons open the configured website; bundled local text is shown when a valid destination is not configured. These browser and system actions are separate from background app networking.
