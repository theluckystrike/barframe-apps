# ShelfDock Support

Click the tray icon to open ShelfDock. Drop files, folders, text, or images onto the open window. The icon opens the window and does not accept drops. There is no automatic cursor-following panel.

Drag a row into a compatible destination app. Use the row menu for Copy to Clipboard, Share, Quick Look, Move to Group, or Remove. Share services depend on system availability. All displays every item; select a named group to add new drops to that group. Use New Group to create a group, and Reset to clear a search with no matches.

Choose Sample to test every shelf action with bundled files, text, and an image. Sample has its own temporary store. Live restores your saved shelf, and discards Sample changes. No private credentials or account are required. Sharing to a recipient requires the selected system service to be available.

Settings opens with Command-comma or the shelf menu. Launch at login reflects the system service status; an approval message appears when System Settings requires approval. Clear items after drag out runs only after the destination accepts a copy. Cancelled drags retain the item. Undo cleared drag restores the complete payload. Clear Shelf deletes items and Undo history without deleting original files.

A Missing hint means the original is unavailable. Restore it or remove the entry. A Permission hint means you should remove the entry and drop the original again. Reopen the row action after restoring a file. Bookmarks are refreshed when stale references resolve. Text and image payloads are stored in the container; references point to originals. The app container may survive deleting the app.

The app performs no analytics or background uploads. Sharing can send data to a device or service when you choose it. For help, open an issue at https://github.com/theluckystrike/barframe-apps/issues or email support@barframe.com. Copyright © 2026 Michael Lip.
