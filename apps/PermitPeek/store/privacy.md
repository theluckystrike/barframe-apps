PermitPeek privacy policy

PermitPeek stores watched application paths, security scoped bookmarks, bundle identities, versions, signing teams, declared entitlements and acknowledged baselines locally on your Mac. It inspects only the main executable signature after you choose an app or request a rescan. It does not read documents, inspect traffic, collect analytics, upload app inventories or contact a server. No network entitlement is requested.

Sample mode uses separate fictional data. Delete Sample watches only clears temporary examples. Delete all live watches and history in Settings erases saved live inspection data even while Sample is enabled. Removing a live watch deletes its local history without changing its application bundle.

If saved data cannot be read, writes remain paused. Retry saved data after restoring a readable file, or explicitly delete all live watches and history to reset it. The unreadable file is kept until you request deletion. Privacy and support pages open in your browser only when requested.
