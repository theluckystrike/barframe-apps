# MeetNext Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-14

MeetNext processes calendar events locally. It has no analytics, advertising, tracking, developer account or developer-operated data collection.

## Calendar access
With your permission, MeetNext reads event titles, times, URLs, locations and notes from the selected calendars for today and tomorrow. All-day and zero-length events are excluded. Calendar contents are held in memory. Calendar selection identifiers and reminder timing are stored in local preferences. Revoke permission in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Calendars. MeetNext refreshes permission on activation and during periodic refresh, clears live events when access is lost, and clears reminder history.

## External links
Join opens a real event's meeting URL in your browser or meeting app. That destination handles the link under its own privacy policy. Privacy Policy and Support buttons can also open external websites. MeetNext does not upload your calendar to the developer.

## Sample mode
Sample events and sample calendar choices are generated locally without access to your calendars. Sample mode resets when the app quits. Sample Join shows a preview without opening a meeting or needing credentials.

## Reminders and retention
Reminders are off by default. Enabling them requests notification permission. Notifications may show meeting titles on your Mac. Event identifiers and reminder times used to avoid duplicate reminders remain only in memory for up to two days, and are cleared when you quit, change calendar selection, switch Sample mode, revoke access or choose Never. Clear reminder history in Settings also removes pending and delivered MeetNext notifications. Earlier versions' persisted reminder history is deleted at launch. Calendar selection and reminder preferences remain locally until changed or the app container is removed. Launch at login is managed by macOS.

## Contact
Use the Support button for help. No data is collected, linked to you, or used for tracking.
