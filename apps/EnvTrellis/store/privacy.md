# EnvTrellis privacy policy

EnvTrellis processes explicitly selected environment files entirely on your Mac. File contents are read transiently to determine key names, presence and whether a value is empty. Values are not displayed, saved, hashed, logged or included in reports. No input is executed or expanded.

Saved projects, environment labels, rules, file metadata, summaries and scoped access bookmarks stay in the app sandbox container. Your source files remain at their selected locations and are never edited or deleted. Clear local data removes app records and Sample fixtures without deleting your files.

The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, telemetry, cloud sync or automatic network requests. No developer data collection occurs. Explicitly copying a report places only the report text on the system clipboard. Other apps may read that clipboard and your subsequent sharing is your choice.

Sample fixtures are invented and visibly labeled. Sample and Live results remain separate. Opening Privacy Policy or Support starts your browser at the product website and the browser makes an ordinary web request.
