StepQuay

Resume your next small task

Keep your next action, remaining effort and saved resume note together in a native Mac panel.

  • Create an ordered queue with your own effort estimates.
  • Interrupt with a required note and explicitly resume when ready.
  • Keep current controls available while viewing the queue.
  • Preview consecutive actions against your available time.
  • Continue, revise or defer overdue work without automatic completion.
  • Review local completion history and undo completion.

Try Sample to explore fictional tasks without accounts or credentials. English and Spanish interfaces are included.

Support

Command comma opens Settings. Command R resumes, Command I interrupts and Command F finishes. The menu bar button opens the panel if your global shortcut is unavailable.

Read support guidance

Privacy

Tasks, notes and history stay in the app sandbox. The app uses no accounts, analytics, cloud sync or app initiated network requests. Website links open your browser. History expires on launch, retention changes and every minute while open, including Sample mode. Forever keeps records until cleared.
