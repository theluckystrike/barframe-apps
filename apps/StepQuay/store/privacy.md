# StepQuay privacy

StepQuay stores task titles, effort estimates, resume notes, active time, completion records and settings locally in its sandbox container. The developer collects no data. The app has no network entitlement and initiates no network requests. It uses no analytics, tracking, accounts or cloud sync.

Sample fixtures are fictional and kept separate from your saved queue. Switching to Sample pauses a running live action first. The app reads the monotonic system clock for active timing and observes system sleep and wake notifications. Sleep pauses timing and resuming requires a user action. App closure also pauses the saved action on its next launch.

Clear history in Settings removes completed records and their notes without deleting queued actions. A corrupt saved queue is not overwritten. Resetting unreadable storage keeps a local recovery backup. Removing the app's container removes its local data. Launch at login is optional and off by default. A Carbon keyboard shortcut opens the panel without Accessibility access.

Live history expires on launch, when retention changes, and every minute while the app is open, including Sample mode. Forever keeps records until you clear them.

Privacy Policy and Support links open the default browser when the website address is finalized. Otherwise these buttons display bundled local documents. The browser then contacts the website under its own privacy settings. No information about your tasks is sent with these links.
