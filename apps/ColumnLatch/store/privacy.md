ColumnLatch privacy policy

ColumnLatch processes selected CSV files on your device. No source file is changed. No source rows are stored after a check. Saved supplier rules, file access bookmarks and value redacted summaries remain in the sandbox container. Column names and contract names appear in reports. Allowed value rules are stored locally. Reports redact cell values and allowed value contents.

There are no accounts, analytics, advertisements, cloud services or automatic network requests. The app has no network entitlement. Data Not Collected applies to this app. Copying a report places the displayed evidence on the system clipboard. Other apps may access clipboard content. Opening Support or Privacy opens your browser and causes its normal web request. Delete local data in Settings removes rules, bookmarks and summaries without deleting source files.
