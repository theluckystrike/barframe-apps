# Starglass Support

Click the moon menu bar icon to see the score, hourly cloud timeline and upcoming nights. Open Settings with the Settings button or Command-comma. Search for a city and select a result. Search text and selected coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo; no location permission is needed.

Try Sample is always available in the panel and requires no accounts or network. Use live forecast exits Sample. Sample Springs is a fictional example city. Night rows are comparisons, not buttons. Tonight shows moon illumination; phase names, moon rise/set and planet positions are not displayed.

Refresh retries the forecast. Automatic refresh runs periodically, on wake and at day changes. Times use the city timezone shown in the header. Cached data is labelled offline or stale; expired data can have no available nights. Forecast scores and local astronomy are approximate planning aids, not guarantees of observing conditions.

Notifications are off by default. Enable them in Settings and permit them in macOS. Disabling cancels pending notifications. Clear saved city and forecast deletes those local records and cancels notifications.

Forecast data: Open-Meteo, https://open-meteo.com/ . Starglass is free and operated as a non-commercial project. Contact: support@barframe.com
