HushKey Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

HushKey is a microphone mute utility for Mac. It does not record, transmit, store, or collect any audio or personal data.

What the app accesses

  • Audio device settings — HushKey reads and writes your microphone's system mute state (CoreAudio device properties) so the shortcut, menu, and push-to-talk can work. It never opens an audio input stream and never captures samples.
  • Preferences — your shortcut choice, badge and Sample-mode toggles, and per-device volume memory are stored locally in the app's sandbox container. Nothing leaves your Mac.

Retention and deletion

Preferences and device memory remain on this Mac until you reset them. In Settings, choose “Reset Local Preferences and Device Memory” to clear saved shortcut, display and mode preferences and remembered device volumes. This does not change launch-at-login registration; turn that off separately in Settings.

No network access

The app makes no network connections. The only URLs in the app are links to this policy and the support page, opened in your browser only when you click them.

No third parties

No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are included.
