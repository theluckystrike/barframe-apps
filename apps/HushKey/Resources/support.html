HushKey Support

HushKey mutes and unmutes your Mac's default microphone with one global shortcut.

Quick start

  1. Click the 🔒/🎙 icon in the menu bar to mute or unmute.
  2. Press ⇧⌥M anywhere to toggle with the global shortcut (changeable in Settings).
  3. Enable Push-to-talk in Settings to hold the shortcut and talk, release to re-mute.

Try it without a microphone

Settings → Sample mode simulates three microphones so every control can be exercised safely. No audio is ever recorded.

FAQ

The shortcut does nothing. Another app may claim it. Pick a different key combination in Settings → Mute shortcut.

Some devices can't be muted. Devices without a mute control are silenced by setting input volume to 0; the previous volume is remembered and restored on unmute.

Does it record me? No. HushKey never opens an audio input stream.
