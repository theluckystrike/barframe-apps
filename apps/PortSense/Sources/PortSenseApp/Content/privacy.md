# PortSense Privacy Policy

PortSense reads charger, battery and USB information locally. No account is required. The app does not collect personal data, track users, include analytics, or send hardware information to the developer.

Charger history stays in memory and resets on quit. Charger rating and the built-in-device preference are stored locally. Launch at Login uses the system login-item service and is off by default. Sample mode uses neutral examples and does not read hardware. The app uses no protected-folder permissions.

Privacy Policy and Support open bundled text when a website is not configured. Once configured, these buttons open the selected page in your browser. Your browser handles that web connection. The local privacy policy remains available without network access.
