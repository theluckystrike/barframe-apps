NameWeave support

Open the menu bar panel and choose See Sample to explore every planning feature without accounts or folder access.

Choose folder grants read access to filenames. Rescan refreshes the supported tree. Hidden items are skipped and packages are single items. Dependency contents are skipped with incomplete coverage. Source files remain unchanged. Resolve candidates makes a suggested plan, then Copy plan places the report on your clipboard.

Command comma opens Settings. Forget root removes saved access and plans. Clear local data restores defaults without deleting files.

If saved access is unavailable, choose the folder again. Support is provided by Michael Lip.
